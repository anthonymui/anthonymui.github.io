Overview

While working as a private swim instructor, I noticed families waiting weeks or even months to be matched with an instructor. Since swim lessons occur mainly in the summer, these delays led to frustration and children missing a limited window to learn a critical life skill.

I founded Swim Shark Academy to reduce customer wait times and create a faster, more reliable experience. The hypothesis was simple: if families could be matched with a qualified instructor within seven days, satisfaction and conversion would increase.

The Problem

The traditional instructor-matching process lacked speed and transparency. Demand existed but wasn't being fulfilled. Four pain points stood out:

  • Long wait times: families waited weeks before being matched with anyone.
  • No transparency: customers had no visibility into the matching process or timeline.
  • Summer urgency: the seasonal window for lessons meant delays had outsized consequences.
  • No structured intake: there was no system to capture location, skill level, or availability upfront.

Solution & Execution

Rather than spending months building infrastructure, I launched fast with simple tools and iterated from real signal.

  • Structured intake form: designed a signup flow capturing location, availability, skill level, and goals to improve match quality. Iterated 5+ times based on direct user feedback, reducing form completion time by ~25%.
  • Backend matching system: built a centralized system to manage client and instructor data and streamline scheduling logistics.
  • AI-assisted contracting: used AI to draft and iterate instructor hiring contracts, compressing multi-day drafting cycles into hours and accelerating onboarding.
  • Targeted outreach: led marketing campaigns to acquire early customers and grow the instructor roster to three.

Key Decisions & Tradeoffs

"Prioritizing speed over perfection: with summer approaching, I chose to launch quickly rather than spend time building a polished website."

  • Google Forms over a custom site: allowed me to validate demand before investing in infrastructure. The right call given the timeline.
  • A/B tested the first lesson structure: experimented with free vs. paid initial lessons to balance quality assurance with conversion.
  • Simplified pricing to reduce friction: launched with three clear packages (6, 12, 18 lessons) and added a 24-lesson discount option after observing demand from highly committed families.
  • Simple referral incentive: a 10% referral discount enabled fast implementation while driving organic word-of-mouth without complex infrastructure.

Reflection

Swim Shark taught me that understanding user pain points and optimizing for speed in the early stages of a product matters more than polish. The hardest decisions weren't technical, they were about what to cut and what to validate first.

If I were to build this again, I would implement automated scheduling for clients and instructors to reduce operational overhead, and leverage AI tooling across the entire workflow to accelerate iteration and decision-making.
